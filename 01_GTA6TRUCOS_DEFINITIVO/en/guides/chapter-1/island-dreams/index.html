Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Kenton hires Jason to guide a bachelor-party dive. The outing is cover for a treasure search at a shipwreck and a rescue attempt for Dobbs.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Meet Kenton at his shop and boat to the dive site.
  2. Equip the scuba gear and lead the group through the trench.
  3. Use the metal detector in the search area to find three treasures.
  4. Free Dobbs from beneath the grate and head back toward the boat.
  5. Choose whether to use the harpoon on the shark and escort the survivors.

Practical tips

  • Watch the oxygen meter, though the route gives you time if you keep moving.
  • Move toward the detector’s signal until it turns green.
  • The harpoon takes two hits to drive the shark away.

Related side activities

Integrated activity: the mission is also Kenton’s first major treasure dive; this one-off content does not need a separate side guide.

Secrets and rewards

Three treasures are near the wreck. Saving Dobbs changes the final dialogue; letting the shark reach him changes the scene, according to the guide.
