Mission overview

Jason needs painkillers while he recovers. Lucia asks around the motel and joins Vicki on a trip through Port Gellhorn to find Dunk and obtain medication.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Ask Zach, near reception, where to find pills.
  2. Knock on Room 10 and speak to Vicki.
  3. Pay $50 for the pills and take them to Jason.
  4. Return to Vicki’s room; the pills are not what Jason needs.
  5. Go with Vicki to Delights and follow her through the club.
  6. Visit Dunk’s trailer. When he is not there, head to the boatyard and speak to his friends.
  7. Go to the car wash and confront the Kolts.
  8. You can choose to defuse the situation in the dialogue; then open the shutter and follow Dunk.
  9. Drive Dunk back to his trailer.

Practical tips

  • Choosing “Defuse” twice can avoid a fight at the car wash, according to the linked walkthrough.
  • The first pills are not the medication Jason needs; the mission continues after you return to Vicki.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

Vicki pays the $50 back through BuckMe. Dunk becomes a contact for the next part of the search.
