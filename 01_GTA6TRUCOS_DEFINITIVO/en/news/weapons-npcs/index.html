GTA VI changes weapon carrying: NPCs will react to what you show

September 6, 2026 · GTA6Trucos

Rob Nelson has explained new details about GTA VI's weapon system. Showing a long gun or carrying a weapon openly can trigger different NPC reactions, from backing away to calling the police.

What it means for GTA VI

This new material reinforces Rockstar's focus on a more reactive and detailed world. Rather than simply adding content, GTA VI is connecting the way you play with NPC reactions, storytelling and everyday life across Leonida.
