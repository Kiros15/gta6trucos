Spoilers: mission summary and objectives.

Mission overview

Once Jason has recovered, the pair go out for breakfast and rob several businesses before escaping the police.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Wait for Jason’s call after completing Slam Dunk and return to the motel.
  2. Drive to Hank’s Waffles.
  3. Enter with your weapon drawn, threaten the staff and collect the cash from the register.
  4. Repeat the robbery at the liquor store and thrift store.
  5. Continue to the next businesses in the sequence.
  6. Return to the vehicle and escape the wanted level.

Practical tips

  • Aim at the clerk to make them open the register, then collect the money before the timer expires.
  • Dirt tracks and side roads help break line of sight during the chase.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

Unlocks the Trust achievement and makes Jason playable again. Both protagonists receive a personal vehicle.
