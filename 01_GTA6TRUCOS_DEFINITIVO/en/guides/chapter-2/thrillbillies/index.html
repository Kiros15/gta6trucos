Mission overview

Wyman tells you about the Mud Club and its dirt races. The activity introduces Billy and his group of riders in Port Gellhorn.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Go to Thrillbilly Mud Club, marked after Coffee Break.
  2. Enter the souvenir shop and buy outfits for Jason and Lucia.
  3. Leave the shop to meet Billy and his crew.
  4. Rent a bike from Roostere’s Rentals.
  5. Head to the organiser and enter the race.
  6. Finish ahead of the last rider to impress Billy.

Practical tips

  • The Sanchez is faster than the Manchez, but costs more to rent.
  • You do not need to win to complete the mission; just avoid finishing last.
  • First place can pay up to $2,500.

Related side activities

This is part of Billy’s optional side-quest chain.

Rewards and unlocks

This is the first job in Billy’s optional questline and unlocks contact for Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab.
