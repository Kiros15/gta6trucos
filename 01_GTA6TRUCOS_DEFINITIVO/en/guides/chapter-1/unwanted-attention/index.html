Spoiler summary

Cal and Jason head to Hamlet to recover money Brian left in an impounded Imponte Phoenix. The job introduces police searches and ways to reduce how recognizable you and the car are.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Meet Cal in Key Lento and drive to the Hamlet impound.
  2. Enter the lot and locate the red Phoenix.
  3. Unhook it with the tow truck and escape the search area.
  4. Take the car to Pay ’n’ Spray and change its appearance.
  5. Deliver the Phoenix to Brian’s garage and collect the money.
  6. Change clothing or accessories to complete the objective.

Practical tips

  • The police search is scripted as part of the mission; keep the car intact and leave the marked area.
  • The respray color does not change the outcome.

Related side activities

Integrated tip: the WAiNK app can scan nearby vehicles and turn exploration into collection progress.

Secrets and rewards

The guide lists a $150 reward. Respraying the car and changing clothes are separate ways to lower recognition.
