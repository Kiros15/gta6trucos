GTA VI · STORY WALKTHROUGH

Chapter 2: Paradise Lost

Follow the story missions in Port Gellhorn, from the Starlet Motel to the move to Vice City. This guide separates the 11 core story missions from Billy’s two optional jobs and the progression tasks between them.

Before Checking Out: final checklist

The final mission requires $15,000 and the required story jobs. Starting it removes the Starlet Motel as your home base. Save manually first and check the motel for collectibles and Wyman’s encounter. You can return to Port Gellhorn later, but the motel room is no longer your base.

Story missions and optional Billy jobs

The order of some objectives can vary depending on which jobs you complete first. Use the in-game story list if a marker appears in a different order.

Progression tasks between missions

The chapter also includes short tasks that are not counted as separate headline story missions in every list: Take Care of Jason / Play Nurse, Overnight Charges, New Look, How Do I Look?, Painkiller Supplier, Behind on Payments and Prepare for Vice City. They appear as the chapter advances and may unlock in a different order. Optional activities include Kyle’s McKittrick Farm and chemical-collection jobs, the pawn shop and bowling alley robberies, the impounded vehicle, and Billy’s chain.

Useful things to do before leaving

Check the Starlet Motel for the Victor Maravilla and Bryson Powers cards, the Vicki card and the magazines at reception and the cafeteria. Help Wyman with his car in the motel car park. If Lucia bought the necklace in Chapter 1, pay Linda the $900 room bill during Behind on Payments to recover it.

Counting note

Mission lists differ because some count only the 11 core story missions, while others include Billy’s two optional jobs and the short progression tasks. This index labels the 11 core missions and the two Billy activities separately rather than presenting every task as a main mission.
