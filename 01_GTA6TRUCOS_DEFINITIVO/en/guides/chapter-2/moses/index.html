Mission overview

Vicki recommends Moses, a mechanic who can help Lucia get a vehicle. The mission introduces car scanning and registering stolen vehicles.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Drive a car to Moses’ garage in Port Gellhorn.
  2. Follow him to the yard with several vehicles.
  3. Open the Waink app and scan the cars to check for trackers.
  4. Pick up the Slim Jim tool next to Moses.
  5. Use the Slim Jim to unlock the Montagne XL by following the tension indicator.
  6. Return to the garage and speak to Moses about registering the vehicle.
  7. If your car has a tracker, find another untracked vehicle on a main road and return.
  8. Change the vehicle’s colour and register it as your personal car.

Practical tips

  • Bring an untracked vehicle to avoid having to find another one.
  • Advanced trackers require tools unlocked later in the story.
  • Moses will not accept cars stolen too close to his garage.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

Unlocks the Slim Jim, Scooter Bros app, personal vehicle registration and the ability to sell untracked cars to Moses.
