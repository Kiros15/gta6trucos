GTA VI Extended Look reveals a huge amount of new gameplay

August 27, 2026

Rockstar has released the GTA VI Extended Look, captured entirely on PlayStation 5. It shows new Vice City and Leonida scenes, combat, driving, activities and a much broader look at the world.
