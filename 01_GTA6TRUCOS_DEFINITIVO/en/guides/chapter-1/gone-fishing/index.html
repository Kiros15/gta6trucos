Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

A package pickup at sea goes wrong, sending Jason into Kingfishers territory to get Donnie out. The mission introduces Cal, the seaplane and Jason’s work for Brian.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Follow the plane and collect the packages from the water with Cal.
  2. Reach the swamp and observe the meeting through binoculars.
  3. Search the camp and locate the shack where Donnie is held.
  4. Free Donnie, get through the close-quarters fight and escape to the seaplane.
  5. Take off, land on the water by the marina and drive Cal home.
  6. Continue to Jason’s first house.

Practical tips

  • For a stealthier approach, wait for guards to move away before advancing toward the shack.
  • When flying, slow down and land on the water before taxiing into the marina.

Related side activities

Related activities: binocular animal studies and Kenton’s later dives fit naturally after this introduction to the coast. Neither is required to advance the story. GTABase also marks Get Shirley’s Rum and Steal the Scooter as missable optional missions; complete them when offered if you want to keep them.

Secrets and rewards

GTABase notes optional cash in a trailer during the escape; collect it before boarding the plane if you want the extra. The listed mission payout is $100–$220 depending on how it plays out.
