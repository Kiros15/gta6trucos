Mission overview

Vicki proposes robbing the safe at Delights, the club where she works. Entry and some objectives change depending on your approach.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Go to Delights when Vicki contacts you.
  2. Find a way into the club’s backstage area. If playing as Jason, the main entrance may be restricted during Ladies’ Night.
  3. Choose whether Jason joins the stage dance or refuses.
  4. Reach the safe room and take the cash.
  5. Fight your way out of the club.
  6. Escape the bikers chasing you and meet Vicki to hand over the money.

Practical tips

  • The dance choice changes some scenes and objectives, but not the overall outcome.
  • If you refuse to dance, the linked walkthrough lists 2008 as the safe code.
  • If you dance, an extra objective to buy clothes for Jason may appear.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

Vicki sends $10,000 through BuckMe the following day, helping towards the savings goal for Vice City.
