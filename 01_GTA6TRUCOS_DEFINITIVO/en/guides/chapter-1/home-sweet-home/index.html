Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Lucia returns to Jason’s house for his belongings but finds it trashed and burning. After escaping, a police manhunt begins through Key Lento.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Enter and fight off the intruders.
  2. Leave the house before it explodes.
  3. Move crouched and quietly toward the road.
  4. Steal a car or continue on foot until you leave the search area.

Practical tips

  • Do not waste time looking for the cash: the guide says there is no time to reach it.
  • The guide notes a photo of Tomás by the door that can be collected.

Related side activities

Missable secret: calling Andy and reaching her car without being spotted unlocks Andy’s Jacket, according to GTABase.

Secrets and rewards

The guide lists Tomás’ photo and Andy’s Jacket as associated extras. The escape leads directly into Discharge Time.
