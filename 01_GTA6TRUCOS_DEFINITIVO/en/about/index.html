About GTA6Trucos

GTA6Trucos is an independent site dedicated to collecting, explaining and analysing public information about Grand Theft Auto VI. We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

How we work

  • We prioritise announcements, pages and materials published by official sources.
  • We write original articles and clearly label analysis and speculation.
  • We include publication and update dates so readers can judge how current each article is.
  • We link to primary sources when a claim depends on an external publication.
Editorial separation: “Confirmed” describes official information; “Analysis” is our interpretation; “Theory” is a hypothesis and must not be confused with an announcement.

Corrections and contact

If you find a broken link, outdated fact or incorrect attribution, contact us using the information published on the site. We review corrections and update articles when necessary.

Transparency

Some pages may contain clearly identified affiliate links. They do not change the price for users and help support the project.
