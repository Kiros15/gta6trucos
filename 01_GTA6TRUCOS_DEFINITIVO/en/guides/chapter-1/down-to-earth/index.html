Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Brian meets Jason at the marina for a smuggling job. Lucia comes along: they clean a seaplane, collect a load, make several drops and hurry back to avoid a parole problem.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Meet Brian at the marina and pressure-wash the boat.
  2. Fly to Bobcat Bay and retrieve the package from the water.
  3. Resolve the confrontation with the smugglers.
  4. Land at the three drop points and release the packages.
  5. Return home, hide the goods in the washing machine and help Lucia with the officer.

Practical tips

  • Land on the water at the marked points for the seaplane sections.
  • You can incapacitate enemies instead of killing them; the mission supports either approach.

Related side activities

Integrated side content: meditation unlocks after the mission, opening Meditation Spot exploration. An optional search for lost packages in the swamp is also related to this job.

Secrets and rewards

The guide lists $500 and up to $800 more for selling leftover cocaine to Donnie; the first Meditation Spot also unlocks.
