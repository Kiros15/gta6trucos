GTA VI Meets the NBA: Miami Heat Will Host 'A Night in Vice City'

Vice City is moving from the game into a real NBA arena on November 18, just one day before GTA VI's planned launch.

Original GTA6Trucos editorial cover; not an official NBA image or game screenshot.

Miami becomes Vice City

The Miami Heat will host the Milwaukee Bucks at Kaseya Center during A Night in Vice City. The collaboration will bring Rockstar's fictional city identity into an official game through a special court, reimagined uniforms and themed entertainment.

The real Miami as an extension of the game

Vice City began as Rockstar's interpretation of Miami; now the real city is temporarily adopting elements of the fiction. The Welcome to Vice City sign installed on Kaseya Center had already previewed this crossover.

Launch eve

The event takes place November 18. Rockstar officially continues to list GTA VI for November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Why it matters

This goes beyond courtside advertising: a Miami sports franchise will temporarily adapt its presentation to the GTA VI universe, creating one of the clearest real-world links between Vice City and its inspiration.

CONFIRMED: November 18, Heat–Bucks, special court, reimagined uniforms and Vice City-themed entertainment.

ANALYSIS: it looks like a centerpiece of the final marketing push.

NOT CONFIRMED: no GTA VI in-game rewards have been announced for attending.
