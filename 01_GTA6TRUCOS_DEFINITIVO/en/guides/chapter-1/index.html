GTA VI · STORY GUIDE

Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise

Follow the chapter’s 22 main story missions in chronological order, plus the Prologue. Titles stay in English to match the game.

Before you move on: missables and the chapter lock-in

Complete Lucia’s errands and the home repairs before Coming Home begins. Check Don Gone for its unique item and wrap up any optional Keys content. Recalibration is the point of no return identified by the consulted walkthrough; save to another slot, deposit cash you want to keep and finish optional activities before starting it. Tourist Trap begins the closing sequence and does not return you to free roam before Chapter 2.

Order and progression

The story alternates between Jason and Lucia before bringing them together. If a mission marker differs in your save, follow the in-game journal and Story Recap: some optional tasks may appear as you progress.

Notable side activities integrated

We have not made a page for every minor errand. The most useful ones —errands and home upgrades before Coming Home, package searches, Kenton’s dives, Don Gone’s unique item and time-sensitive optional tasks— are included with the related mission.
