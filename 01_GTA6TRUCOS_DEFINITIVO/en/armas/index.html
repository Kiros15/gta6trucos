GTA VI weapons: possible arsenal and combat design

Weapons have always shaped GTA’s sandbox, but their relationship with missions matters more than the number of models. Here are our design hypotheses.

Editorial analysis: promotional facts are separated from opinions and hypotheses; no speculative mechanic is confirmed.

Fewer items, more decisions

Choosing equipment before a job could make preparation meaningful. This is an editorial possibility, not a confirmed inventory limit.

Meaningful customization

Optics, grips and other accessories could affect handling rather than just appearance. Trade-offs would make upgrades more interesting than pure damage increases.

Combat and surroundings

Tight interiors, roads and open terrain should reward different tactics. We do not claim any specific unverified weapon or feature is included.
