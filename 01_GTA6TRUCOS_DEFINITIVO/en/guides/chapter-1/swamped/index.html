Spoiler summary

Jason accompanies Brian to the Kingfishers’ camp. A visit that seems routine turns out to be an ambush and leads to a boat rescue for Lori.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Go to the marina back room and drive with Brian to the camp.
  2. Follow Brian and inspect the pen when he finishes speaking.
  3. Fight through the attack and follow him to the helicopter.
  4. Chase the boat and protect Lori from the air.
  5. Pull alongside Lori’s boat, jump aboard and complete the quick-time prompt.

Practical tips

  • For the jump to the boat, get very close and hold position until the prompt appears.
  • If the engine takes too much damage, the checkpoint restarts.

Related side activities

Integrated activity: this continues Brian’s story thread; the rescue does not need a separate side page.

Secrets and rewards

The guide lists $1,000. Afterwards, telling Lucia what happened with Brian can improve the relationship.
