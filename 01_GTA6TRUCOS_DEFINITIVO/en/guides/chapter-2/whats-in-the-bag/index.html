Mission overview

At the Starlet Motel, Lucia and Jason inspect the bag taken from the visitor centre. The statue and hard drive point to a mystery they cannot solve yet.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Inspect the objects inside the bag.
  2. Turn the small statue over and read the note hidden underneath.
  3. Inspect the hard drive; they cannot access it without a computer.

Practical tips

  • There is no need to look for a computer: the mission ends after checking the note and drive.
  • Remember the note as a story detail for later.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

This opens Paradise Lost and lets you play as Lucia in the open world.
