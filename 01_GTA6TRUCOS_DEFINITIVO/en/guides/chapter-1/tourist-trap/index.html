Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Mattia offers the couple a seemingly simple job: pick up a bag. The handoff turns out to be a police trap and transforms the end of the chapter into an escape.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Speak with Lucia on the beach when you are ready to move on.
  2. Go to the visitor center and collect the bag from the marked locker.
  3. Follow the arrest and interrogation sequence prompts.
  4. As Lucia, cover Jason and get him to the boat.
  5. Escape by water to Cal’s house.

Practical tips

  • WARNING: this mission starts a sequence that does not return to free roam before the next chapter. Finish anything you care about first.
  • During the shootout, use cover and preserve health; collect the officer’s shotgun if you need it.

Related side activities

This is the narrative threshold to the finale. Side activities belong before this sequence, not inside it.

Secrets and rewards

The guide identifies this job as the last chance to free roam in the chapter and warns that pocket cash and the Key Lento house are lost.
