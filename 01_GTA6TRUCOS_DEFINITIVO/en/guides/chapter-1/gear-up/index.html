Spoiler summary

Jason visits Ammu-Nation to pick up a longarm. The Kingfishers ambush him outside; the consulted walkthrough says either surviving or being taken down ends the mission.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Go to the Ammu-Nation near the house.
  2. Speak with Andrew and buy the available shotgun or tactical rifle.
  3. Resolve the Kingfishers encounter outside.

Practical tips

  • The shotgun costs less; the tactical rifle is the more precise option if you already have enough cash.
  • You do not need to chase the attackers; the goal is to get through the encounter.
