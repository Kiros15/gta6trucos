Vehicle upgrades: five improvements we would love to see

Customization is familiar territory for GTA. In GTA VI it could become deeper if upgrades changed how vehicles behave across Leonida.

Editorial analysis: promotional facts are separated from opinions and hypotheses; no speculative mechanic is confirmed.

Build for the terrain

Rain tires, off-road suspension and gearing choices would fit a map with cities, coastline and rural routes. These are design suggestions, not announced upgrades.

Visible and useful changes

Bodywork, lights and mechanical parts could create meaningful differences without pushing everyone toward the same optimized car.

Style and risk

A flashy vehicle might attract attention, while a discreet one might suit certain jobs. This connection between aesthetics and consequences remains speculative.
