Pipework

Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Brian sends Jason to collect from Mandy. She makes payment conditional on fixing her blocked sink, turning a rent call into a home repair.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Speak with Mandy outside her house.
  2. Follow her inside and find the plunger in the bathroom.
  3. Go to the kitchen and interact with the sink.
  4. Use the plunger, then the wrench, to fix the pipe.

Practical tips

  • The needed item is in the bathroom across the hall from the bedroom.
  • Check the street on your way out: you can pick up the cigar box Mandy throws.

Related side activities

Secret/reward: pick up the cigar box from the street to get the Macizo Cigar.

Secrets and rewards

The Macizo cigar box is left on the street after the scene; the guide does not list a cash reward.
