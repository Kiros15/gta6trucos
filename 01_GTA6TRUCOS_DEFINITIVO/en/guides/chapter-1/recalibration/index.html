Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Jason and Lucia try to cheer Cal up with beer, fishing and a meal at home. The consulted guide identifies this mission as the start of the story stretch without free-roam that leads into the next chapter.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Buy a six-pack and take Jason to Cal.
  2. Go with Cal to the fishing spot and catch a fish using the prompts.
  3. Return to the dock and cook the three pieces.
  4. Take Lucia home to finish the scene.

Practical tips

  • WARNING: the guide marks this as Chapter 1’s point of no return. Before starting, save to another slot, deposit cash you want to keep and finish any time-sensitive optional content.
  • The guide recommends keeping at least $5,000 in Family Savings.

Last chance to wrap up free-roam content in the Keys: optional activities, Donnie’s car missable and tasks tied to Kenton or Cal. Check your in-game journal for what is still active in your save.
