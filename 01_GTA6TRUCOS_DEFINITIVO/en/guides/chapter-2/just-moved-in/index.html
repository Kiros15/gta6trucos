Mission overview

The couple needs supplies for their room at the Starlet Motel. Lucia crosses the road to Alpha Convenience Store to pick up essentials.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Cross the road from the Starlet Motel to the gas station.
  2. Enter Alpha Convenience Store.
  3. Buy the eight marked items: soda, protein bars, fruit, soup, gauze, bandanas, hair clippers and a makeup bag.
  4. Return to the motel room with the supplies.

Practical tips

  • Items are charged as you pick them up; there is no need to visit the cashier.
  • If your minimap is disabled, temporarily enable markers to locate the products.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

The mission increases the protagonists’ relationship.
