Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Jason takes his personal Ganado to Coastal Auto Care to repair the engine and learn the Pay ’n’ Spray customization options.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Drive the Ganado from home to Coastal Auto Care.
  2. Talk to Joey and take the car into the garage.
  3. Select the full engine repair.
  4. Open cosmetics, choose a respray and leave the shop.

Practical tips

  • The engine repair listed in the guide is free.
  • Avoid spending heavily on permanent upgrades yet: the guide notes the Ganado is replaced later.

Related side activities

Related system: this is the garage tutorial. The Ultimate Edition may include an optional free body kit; it is not required.

Secrets and rewards

The respray color is up to you. The guide lists two unlocks; the Ultimate kit is an edition bonus.
