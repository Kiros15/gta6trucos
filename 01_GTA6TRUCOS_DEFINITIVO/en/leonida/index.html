Leonida beyond Vice City: how exploration could evolve

Promotional locations contrast urban spaces, coastline and wilderness. We examine what those settings could mean for mission design.

Editorial analysis: promotional facts are separated from opinions and hypotheses; no speculative mechanic is confirmed.

Different rhythms across the map

A dense city supports pursuits and chance encounters; the coast offers marine routes and wilderness could enable exploration and side activities. Their depth is not yet confirmed.

A reactive world

We would like traffic, wildlife and atmosphere to vary by location and time. Promotional scenes alone do not establish gameplay systems.

Exploration with purpose

Great open worlds reward curiosity with small stories, not just collectibles. That is how we plan to assess Leonida when the game is available.
