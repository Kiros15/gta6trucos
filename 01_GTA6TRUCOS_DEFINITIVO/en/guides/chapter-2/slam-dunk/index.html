Spoilers: mission summary and objectives.

Mission overview

Dunk tries to secure stronger painkillers for Jason. Lucia joins him in a deal that ends in a confrontation with the Kolts.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Go to Dunk’s trailer when he calls and join him while he waits for Doctor Bill.
  2. Follow Dunk and Bill to the docks.
  3. Drive slowly behind the contact guiding you through the Kolts’ compound.
  4. When the shooting starts, reverse and escape the compound.
  5. Lose the pursuers and pick up Bill when he leaves the vehicle.
  6. Drive to the airstrip southwest of Port Gellhorn.
  7. Defend yourself from the Kolts and search the main building for Dunk.
  8. Drive Dunk back to his trailer.

Practical tips

  • At the airstrip, use cover and advance carefully; enemies are inside and outside the buildings.
  • Keep Zombix ready to restore health during the shootout.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

You obtain Jason’s painkillers and Dunk becomes an unlocked fence. The next day he sends $1,700 through BuckMe and can mark two robbery locations.
