Spoilers: mission summary and objectives.

Mission overview

Billy contacts you again to help rob an armoured van. The job takes place on dirt bikes and requires you to stay with his crew.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Wait for Billy’s message after completing Thrillbillies.
  2. Go to the meeting point and interact with the bench to meet him.
  3. Get on the Sanchez dirt bike and follow Billy.
  4. Chase the armoured van with the crew until it crashes.
  5. Go to the rear doors and plant the explosive charge.
  6. Deal with the security guard and collect the bag.
  7. Get back on your bike and follow Billy to the meeting point on the hill.

Practical tips

  • Focus on riding and staying on the bike during the chase; you do not need to shoot the van.
  • You can reduce risk by avoiding an unnecessary fight with the guard.

Related side activities

This is part of Billy’s optional side-quest chain.

Rewards and unlocks

The linked walkthrough lists a $2,000 reward. The Sanchez bike can be useful for later off-road activities.
