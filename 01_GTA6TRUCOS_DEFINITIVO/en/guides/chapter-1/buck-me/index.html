Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Lucia needs to receive her community-service pay. At Fleeca they learn they cannot open a standard account and switch to the BuckMe finance app.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Meet Lucia at the Fleeca branch.
  2. Speak with the teller and then the manager.
  3. Open BuckMe on the phone after the scene.
  4. Move the welcome bonus from checking into Family Savings.
  5. Choose whether to tell Lucia about your savings.

Practical tips

  • Family Savings cannot be withdrawn, but the guide says it is protected from loss.
  • The first savings target listed is $5,000.

Related side activities

Integrated money tip: use Family Savings for goals and deposit cash at ATMs if you want to avoid losing pocket money on death.

Secrets and rewards

The choice to tell Lucia about savings affects the relationship, according to the guide. The mission unlocks BuckMe.
