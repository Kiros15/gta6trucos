Mission overview

With the money raised and the required story jobs completed, Jason and Lucia leave the Starlet Motel for Vice City. Their departure turns into an armed escape.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Before starting, make a manual save and finish any motel activities you still want to do.
  2. Once you have at least $15,000 and have completed the required story jobs, return to the Starlet Motel room.
  3. Interact with the bathroom door and pick up the shotgun from the bed.
  4. Fight the corrupt police and regroup with Lucia at the motel cafeteria.
  5. Take control of Lucia when prompted and clear the enemies blocking the exit.
  6. Follow Jason to the road and take the motorcycle when the opportunity appears.
  7. Stay close to Jason’s car during the chase and jump into the vehicle when prompted.
  8. Cover the escape by shooting at police vehicles, preferably aiming for their tyres.

Practical tips

  • This closes the chapter: starting it permanently removes the Starlet Motel as your home base.
  • The linked guide recommends saving beforehand; GTA VI does not allow mission replay.
  • Avoid falling off the motorcycle during the chase. A possible objective bug has been reported if you die during this section; restarting the mission may fix it.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

You need $15,000 to start the move. After the escape you reach Vice City and Chapter 3 begins.
