Don Gone

Brian sends Jason to clear Donnie’s trailer so it looks as though he left town. Gather his belongings and get rid of the car before leaving the area.

ProtagonistJason
Unlock requirementA Brian job in Jason’s chain before the chapter finale.
Reward / extraThe H&L .38 revolver in the trunk is the notable missable item. The guide lists $200 and two unlocks.
Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Brian sends Jason to clear Donnie’s trailer so it looks as though he left town. Gather his belongings and get rid of the car before leaving the area.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Collect the bag in the trailer.
  2. Find and pack the belongings highlighted by the game.
  3. Escape in Donnie’s car when the men from the bar arrive.
  4. Destroy the vehicle to remove the trail.

Practical tips

  • Before destroying the car, open its trunk and collect the H&L .38 revolver; the guide marks it as unique and missable.
  • You can destroy the car with the gas can in the trunk or sink it in the sea.

Related side activities

Related content: Donnie’s Lost Drugs is optional and separate from the main job. Complete it before the point of no return if you want it.

Secrets and rewards

The H&L .38 revolver in the trunk is the notable missable item. The guide lists $200 and two unlocks.
