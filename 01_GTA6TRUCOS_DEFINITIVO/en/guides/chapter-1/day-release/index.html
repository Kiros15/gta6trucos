Spoiler summary

Kat and Mattia invite Jason and Lucia out on the water and later into Vice City. The mission combines a boat trip, jet skis, a party and relationship scenes.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Meet Kat and Mattia at the Rusty Anchor.
  2. Take the speedboat to the yacht.
  3. Complete the jet-ski race.
  4. As Lucia, shoot the champagne bottles.
  5. Find Mattia and follow him to the motel.
  6. Visit the Nine1Nine and follow the party prompts before returning to the Keys.

Practical tips

  • Winning the race is not required to progress.
  • On the yacht, look for the optional sunglasses before leaving; the guide says they are not available later.

Related side activities

Related activity: Clock Out is a time-sensitive optional task tied to Lucia; the consulted list marks it optional, so take it when the game offers it before progressing.

Secrets and rewards

Lucia’s dress is cosmetic; the yacht sunglasses are the notable collectible and can be missed.
