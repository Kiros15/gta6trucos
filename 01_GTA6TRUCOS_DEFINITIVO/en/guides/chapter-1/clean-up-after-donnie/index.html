Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

A lost package from Donnie’s failed drop is transmitting near a fishing spot. Jason follows the trail to a shack guarded by the Kingfishers.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Find the fisherman in the marked area and locate the package.
  2. Inspect it to learn where its contents went.
  3. Take the boat to the shack farther downriver.
  4. Recover the cocaine and bring it to Donnie’s trailer.

Practical tips

  • Use the boat beside the package to follow the river.
  • The $600 reward is sent to the house mailbox later.

Related side activities

No separate side quest: this search resolves the lost-package thread within the main mission.

Secrets and rewards

The guide lists a delayed $600 payout.
