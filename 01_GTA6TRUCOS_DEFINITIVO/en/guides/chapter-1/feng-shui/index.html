Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Jason and Cal spend time at home before Lucia arrives. They rearrange the furniture and try two console minigames.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Enter the house when the marker appears and talk with Cal.
  2. Help move the TV, coffee table, couch and chair.
  3. Spend some time playing Tenebrae Lux.
  4. Let Street Trash III run until the scene ends.

Practical tips

  • The table’s final position does not change the outcome.
  • You do not need to win the minigames; the scene advances with time.

Related side activities

Useful preparation window: GTABase lists optional errands for groceries, flowers, a necklace, a haircut and collecting Lucia’s clothes. Do any you want before Coming Home’s call.

Secrets and rewards

The guide confirms two minigames. Exploring the Tenebrae Lux shed is optional and does not change the mission ending.

What is verified
