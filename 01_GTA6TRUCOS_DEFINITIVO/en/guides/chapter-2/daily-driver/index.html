Mission overview

Moses texts you a job: recover a 1982 Canis Crawler from clients who have not paid.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Read Moses’ message and head to the search area near the old Trader’s Union building.
  2. Find the white Crawler inside the search area.
  3. Deal with the enemies or use an opening to get into the vehicle.
  4. Drive the Crawler to Moses’ garage in Port Gellhorn.

Practical tips

  • The goal is to deliver the vehicle; avoid unnecessary damage to earn the condition bonus.
  • You can approach the search area quietly or make a quick getaway.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

Pays $1,250, with a possible $500 bonus for delivering the vehicle in good condition. Moses later offers a Port Gellhorn garage and the real-estate app for buying garages.
