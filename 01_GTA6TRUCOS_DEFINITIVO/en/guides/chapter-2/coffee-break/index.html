Mission overview

After the robberies, Jason and Lucia decide to keep working together and raise the money needed to move to Vice City.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Go into the motel kitchen with Lucia.
  2. Buy a coffee from the machine for $2.
  3. Listen to the conversation about their next job.
  4. Return to the car and use the partner interaction control to unlock free character switching.
  5. Check the map: several jobs become available and can be tackled in different orders.

Practical tips

  • You can freely switch between Jason and Lucia after this mission.
  • There is no single required order for the jobs that appear next; complete the pending objectives to advance the story.

Related side activities

No separately confirmed side activity is tied directly to this mission.

Rewards and unlocks

Unlocks free character switching. Available jobs include Thrillbillies, the Avery Pawn and Kingpin Bowling robberies, and the impounded-vehicle task.
