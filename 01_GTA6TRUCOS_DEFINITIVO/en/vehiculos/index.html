GTA VI vehicles: what we expect from cars, bikes and boats

Promotional scenes make mobility a major part of Leonida. We consider how driving might evolve while keeping expectations separate from confirmed features.

Editorial analysis: promotional facts are separated from opinions and hypotheses; no speculative mechanic is confirmed.

Different roads, different rides

City avenues, back roads and coastal areas call for different vehicles. We would welcome fast cars with off-road trade-offs, useful off-roaders and boats that unlock alternative routes.

Damage that matters

Progressive damage affecting steering, grip or noise could transform pursuits. This level of simulation has not been confirmed for GTA VI.

Traffic with personality

More varied driver and pedestrian reactions could make escaping about more than speed. Actual traffic systems require verified gameplay evidence.
