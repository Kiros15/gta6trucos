EDITORIAL ANALYSIS

The GTA VI DualSense reinforces the game's launch strategy

October 3, 2026 · Updated: October 3, 2026 · Equipo editorial GTA6Trucos

Launch products do not replace the game, but they help turn a brand into something recognisable. The special DualSense serves exactly that purpose.

We separate official information from our own interpretations. This article does not replace announcements from Rockstar Games.

An object that extends the campaign

A limited controller keeps GTA VI present outside trailers and news. It also brings Vice City's and Leonida's identity into spaces where the game would not normally appear.

What it does not confirm

The controller's design does not prove new gameplay functions, compatibility with specific mechanics or exclusive content. It is a product piece and should be analysed as such.

Why the design matters

The choice of colours, artwork and recognisable elements can become an extension of the visual campaign. For Rockstar, every promotional item helps build a coherent image before launch.
