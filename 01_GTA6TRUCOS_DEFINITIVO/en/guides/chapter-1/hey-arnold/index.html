Hey Arnold!

Brian’s broker Arnold has stopped answering. Jason and Cal track him down; the chase ends at the docks, where Jason must decide what to do with him.

Unlock requirementA Brian mission in Jason’s progression after the early Kingfishers encounters.

Mission spoilers: full summary and objectives.

Spoiler summary

Brian’s broker Arnold has stopped answering. Jason and Cal track him down; the chase ends at the docks, where Jason must decide what to do with him.

Step-by-step objectives

  1. Meet Brian at the pool bar and pick up Cal.
  2. Search for Arnold in the marked Maroon Key area.
  3. Chase his car and follow him on foot to the docks.
  4. Choose whether to kill Arnold or let him go.

Practical tips

  • During the chase, focus on keeping the car on the road; the on-foot pursuit follows.
  • The final choice changes the rating described by the guide, but it reports no later story consequences.

Related side activities

This does not create a separate side mission; Arnold’s choice is part of this mission.

Secrets and rewards

The final choice is irreversible in the scene; GTABase says neither option leaves Arnold available afterward.

What is verified
