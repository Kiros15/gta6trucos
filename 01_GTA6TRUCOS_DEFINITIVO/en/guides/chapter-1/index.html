GTA VI · STORY GUIDE

Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise

The story opens with Lucia in prison and soon shifts to Jason in Leonida. This guide lays out the chapter, summarizes its progression and separates cross-checked details from gameplay advice. Includes spoilers for mission names and structure.

Step-by-step overview

  1. 01

    Begin with Lucia’s prologue at Leonida Penitentiary: learn the controls and the story’s starting point.

  2. 02

    Move into Jason’s early tasks. The chapter alternates jobs, preparation and activities that introduce his routine in Leonida.

  3. 03

    Progress through missions for both protagonists and their contacts. Some tasks may be available in parallel; follow the active marker and the in-game Story Recap.

  4. 04

    The story brings Jason and Lucia together and shifts toward jobs they take on as a team. Complete the main missions the game marks to advance.

  5. 05

    Finish the chapter’s closing sequence and move into the next chapter. The list below follows the order published by the sources consulted.

Practical tips

  • Turn on Fast Walk in the options if walking feels slow; this is a control preference, not a mission requirement.
  • Do not feel pressured to spend or grind money in the opening hours: prioritize objectives and learn the systems before investing in extras.
  • During missions, use checkpoint retry if a mistake leaves you far from the objective.
  • Outside missions, avoid carrying cash and valuables when unnecessary; use deposit or storage options as your save unlocks them.
  • Make a manual save before trying optional activities or choices you may not want to repeat. This is a precaution, not a confirmed chapter requirement.

What is confirmed and what is advice

Recent editorial mission lists confirm the chapter name, its 22 missions and the order of the published titles. The shortened story overview is editorial interpretation based on mission summaries and walkthroughs; follow on-screen prompts if your playthrough differs. Tips are recommendations, not mandatory objectives.
